# Quick start

Get a first position on your own floor in about 15 minutes. You need the
[Android app installed](install.md#android-app) with scan throttling off. No server needed.

## 1. Make a place

1. Open the **Set up** tab and tap **Floor plan**.
2. Give the place a name.
3. **Fastest:** under **Floor plan picture**, choose a picture of your floor (PNG/JPG, or a
   screenshot of a PDF). Tap both ends of something with a known length, type the length,
   and tap **Save**.
   *Or* choose **Warehouse** / **Walkways** and set the number of aisles, their length and the
   space between them.

## 2. Record Wi-Fi

**Set up > Record Wi-Fi anywhere**:

1. Tap the map where you stand.
2. Tap **Walk** and walk slowly in a straight line for 5 to 15 m.
3. Tap the map where you stopped.
4. Repeat along every corridor, both ways. Use **Record** (stand 20 s, turn around once) at
   spots that matter, such as desks or aisle ends.

Turn on the **coverage layer** (layers button). Aim for no grey where people work and no red.

## 3. See yourself

Open the **Live** tab and walk. The blue dot is you; the circle is the uncertainty. If it
says **Near A or B**, stand still a few seconds or record more between those spots.

## 4. Measure

Go to the **Test** tab and follow the [accuracy test](accuracy-test.md). That gives the
real numbers for your floor.

## 5. Optional: traffic demo

Start the [server](install.md#server-backend), fill in the server address in **Settings**, and
turn on **Live > Traffic > Traffic demo**. Set 4 to 8 ghosts on the dashboard. Details:
[Field guide, Part 6](../field-guide.md#part-6-traffic-demo).
